Dark Mode Cartography

Zone A-4 ● Normal
Zone C-2 ▲ High Load

We designed a custom map tiling engine that inverts standard cartography. By darkening the base layer and using neon accents only for active data points, we reduced eye strain for operators working 12-hour shifts in dim control rooms.
