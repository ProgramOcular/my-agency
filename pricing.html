Tactical Data (FAQ)

What is the typical mission timeline?
Most comprehensive identity systems take 4-6 weeks from ignition to launch. Complex web engineering projects generally require 8-12 weeks depending on the scope of the telemetry required.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Affirmative. We offer monthly "Orbit Maintenance" packages to ensure your systems remain operational and secure. This includes server monitoring, content updates, and security patches.
What are the payment protocols?
We typically operate on a 50/50 split: 50% deposit to initiate the project, and the remaining 50% upon final deployment. For Vision-tier projects, we can structure a 3-part milestone payment plan.
