Client: Kinetics Sportswear

Visualizing Momentum

Kinetics is a high-performance apparel brand for urban runners. They needed an identity that felt fast even when standing still.

We moved away from static logos and created a "variable identity system." The logo itself stretches, compresses, and italicizes based on the velocity of the runner wearing it—a data-driven brand mark.
