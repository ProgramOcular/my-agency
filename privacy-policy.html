Privacy Protocols.

Secure transmission established. Ocular is committed to the absolute integrity and protection of your digital footprint.

01. Data Acquisition

We collect specific variables to optimize our design operations. When you initiate collaboration via our "Start a Project" uplink or subscribe to our transmission feeds, we may record:

  • Identity Coordinates (Name)
  • Comms Channel (Email)
  • Organization Metadata
  • Project Parameters (Budget/Timeline)

02. Operational Usage

Captured data is utilized strictly for mission-critical objectives. We do not sell, trade, or leak your tactical data to unauthorized third-party entities.

Project Execution: To calibrate our services to your specific requirements and facilitate ongoing communication regarding your project status.

System Improvement: Analyzing telemetry (website traffic patterns) to refine our digital architecture and user experience.

03. Telemetry Beacons (Cookies)

Our interface utilizes "Cookies" (digital beacons) to maintain session continuity and analyze traffic flow. These are small text files stored on your local drive.

You maintain full command authority over these beacons. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser's security settings, though this may disrupt certain visual functionalities of the Ocular interface.

04. Third-Party Uplinks

Our digital ecosystem may contain hyperlinks to external sectors (partners, tools, or resources). Once you navigate away from the Ocular domain, our protocols no longer apply. We advise reviewing the privacy directives of any external site you visit.

Note: We use secure payment processors (Stripe) and scheduling tools (Calendly) which operate under their own high-security encryption standards.
